Companion App

An app to guide you through your therapeutic journey

  1. Start a path

    You can start many paths, in different times of your life.

  2. Find your needs

    Use the profile test to guide you

  3. What to search for

    Practical points from your test

  4. Asses Therapeutic Alliance

    The best predictor you a good process is a good connection that promotes the work.

  5. Activate the process:
    1. Cards

      Use the cards to work between sessions, increasing your awareness and process.

    2. Journal

      keep track of your sessions and reflections you are having. Writing activates extra processing.

    3. Work through ruptures.

      Therapy that goes deep may have moments of rupture, that when worked with your therapy, can help deeper understanding and healing.

  6. Prepare for an ending.

    When therapy goes well, the ending is planned and help you to understand what has happened and to have a healthy ending of a deep relationshp.
